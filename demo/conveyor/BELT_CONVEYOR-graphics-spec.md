## Plant tag model

- One `NETS[1].ZPA[g]` group instance per 1799ER block covers up to 8 zones; the group input word bits 0..7 are the zone sensors and the output word bits 0..7 are the zone run commands.
- Zone k of a group uses input/output bit k-1 and `NETS[1].ZPA[g].ZONE_STATUS[k]`; group status words start at `CONFIG_ZPA.FIRST_ADDR` (1 in the generated project).

## Layout

- Header strip: design name, controller name, conveyor type, segment/zone/group/block counts, curved zones, spiral rise, scan zones, tracking.
- Left region: top-view conveyor strip (one rectangle per zone, rotated to the segment angle). Belt zones are drawn as a moving belt band; spiral segments show a height badge.
- Zone label BELT1_Z1 above each zone; sensor dot at the leading edge; motor square in the zone centre (belt zones: belt band animation instead of the motor square).
- Scan-zone diamond markers with the scanner name are drawn on top of the conveyor strip.
- Merge Y junctions: main-line and spur lines meet at the merge zone; the granted upstream side is highlighted (main green, spur blue).
- Transfer device symbols on their segment: pusher (square + arrow), rotary table (circle + cross), pop-up transfer (rectangle + left/right arrows) with live state colours.
- Command row: Run / Stop / Reset / Sim buttons; status text on the right (jams, tracked trays, merge grants, active devices).
- Legend: four state swatches (stopped/running/full/jam) plus scanner, belt, merge-grant and device markers.
- Right region: zone tag mapping table (Zone, Block, PLC tag path, Input, Output, IP), then the Tray tracking panel (Slot, Barcode, Zone, State), then the belt drive table (Segment, Run, Fault, Status).
- Zone status values: 0 = normal, 1 = full, 2 = jammed, 4 = out of service, 5 = 24 V loss, 6 = comm loss, 20 = jam acknowledged.
